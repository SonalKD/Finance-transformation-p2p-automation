# Business Case

## Project Overview

This project simulates a Finance Transformation initiative within a growing international company.

The company processes a large number of supplier invoices every month. Parts of the Purchase-to-Pay (P2P) process are manual, requiring Finance employees to compare purchase orders, invoices, approvals and payment information across different files and systems.

The objective of this project is to redesign the process and demonstrate how data, automation and stronger controls can reduce manual work, improve accuracy and provide better visibility to Finance teams.

## Current Business Problem

The existing P2P process contains several manual activities and control weaknesses.

Finance employees spend significant time:

* Checking invoices against purchase orders.
* Identifying invoices without valid PO references.
* Investigating price or quantity differences.
* Checking whether invoices have been approved.
* Identifying possible duplicate invoices.
* Monitoring overdue approvals and payments.
* Preparing exception reports manually.

As transaction volumes increase, these manual activities become difficult to scale and create operational and financial risk.

## Transformation Objective

The Finance Transformation initiative aims to:

* Reduce repetitive manual work.
* Improve invoice-processing efficiency.
* Strengthen financial and operational controls.
* Automatically identify invoice and payment exceptions.
* Improve data quality and traceability.
* Provide Finance teams with actionable reporting.
* Create a scalable process that can support future business growth.

## Proposed Solution

The project will design an automated P2P control and reporting solution that combines supplier, purchase-order, invoice, approval and payment data.

Automated validation and reconciliation rules will identify exceptions such as:

* Duplicate invoices.
* Missing purchase orders.
* Invoice and PO amount mismatches.
* Missing approvals.
* Invalid or incomplete supplier information.
* Late approvals.
* Late payments.

Exceptions will be presented in a structured Finance Exceptions Report so that Finance teams can focus on cases requiring investigation rather than manually reviewing every transaction.

The project will also explore opportunities for intelligent automation and AI-assisted exception triage while maintaining appropriate human oversight and auditability.

## Expected Business Value

The proposed transformation is expected to:

* Reduce manual invoice-review effort.
* Improve control effectiveness.
* Reduce the risk of duplicate or incorrect payments.
* Improve reporting transparency.
* Support faster exception resolution.
* Increase process scalability.
* Allow Finance employees to spend more time on higher-value activities.
